*How do you engage with AI and what does it mean to you? Please consider your different roles, such as personal user, designer/engineer, researcher.*

*Disclaimer: Em dashes (—) have always been part of my writing, predating LLMs. In this case they are not to be seen as an indicator for AI writing.*

AI has become a substantial part of my daily undertakings. In the following I want to cover four aspects of this. First, AI as a developer. Secondly, AI as a designer. Thirdly, AI as a learning companion. And fourthly, all the other daily questions.

## The use of AI as a developer

Large Language Models are better at writing code than any person on this earth. This is true now, might be obvious in hindsight. But even years ago, I was convinced of the opinion that there is no way that any human on Earth can learn coding faster than AI progresses. So naturally, in conversations with people who aren't that integrated into the developer community, the question arises: What are you even doing then? Isn't it we realize that being a developer has never really been about writing syntax, typing on a keyboard. It's been about figuring out complex solutions to a problem. And I couldn't be happier that AI models got there. And also I wouldn't have started studying computer science if AI hadn't come up. It's tool, after all. And it will always remain one. 

Using it, my output increases tenfold in quality. 

## The use of AI as a designer

The use of AI for design work is the complete contrary to the use of AI in development. Output quality is halved whenever and try to extensively rely on AI to design. 

That's because design is subjective. With code, you have test cases that you need to pass at the end. There is a right and a wrong answer. With design, there is not. And at the end of the day, that's the whole point, that the first designer does it differently than the second designer, and vice versa. We lose that touch of personality, but mostly also creativity whenever we're relying on AI. Observation and selection is not the same as creation, and creating something from scratch is a craft that we as humanity should not lose. It's much easier to just say what you like or what you dislike about a proposal, but the end result will always be shaped by that first proposal, no matter how much you change. 

I would love to see AI as a creative partner, but currently, I don't. 

## The use of AI as a learning companion

*I am studying engineering and the following are my observations. Things might be different in other domains.*

Using AI as a learning companion is like having a 24/7 tutor that is not allowed to say no. That's absolutely amazing on paper. Whenever I have a question, the answer is just seconds away. I can get explanations in from many different angles in many different shapes and forms, and I use that to the fullest extend. Learning has never been easier. 

But that's the issue at the same time. Learning is not supposed to be easy. The answer itself is worthless, the hours you spent getting to that answer are where the value's at. The deep thinking that you do when you know you have to somehow figure out the solution to the problem is. And we don't do that anymore. 

Because knowing that the solution or a hint is only seconds away, your mind grows inherintly hesitant to spend hours on something that you could get in seconds. I am therefore unsure what the long-term effects of the use of AI in learning is. 

## All the other daily questions

This is where AI really shines. We're asking ourselves a million questions every day and using Google search finding the answers takes so much longer than it should. As much as I am hesitant to accept using AI as a guide, as much I love not having to click through Cookie banners, different websites, ads, and more. 

The answer is just right there and you can continue with your life. This means I can live more actively and spend less time looking at the screen. Suppose I am in the tram, commuting to university, thinking about the day ahead and a question pops up, I talk into my phone, get an answer in seconds, and can continue looking outside the window. I used to have to spend the rest of the commute on my phone trying to get the answer in Google. 

Ultimately AI will bring humans closer together again, technology will seem to move into the background, while actually being more prevalent than ever.
