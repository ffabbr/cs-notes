There are only two people in this world you need to make proud in your life: your 90-year-old self, and your nine-year-old self. One is a story of history, the other a story of hope.

When you’re 90 years old, you have a lot of experience when looking back. You probably won’t care about money or status, but about what you actually did in your life. What is your heritage?

There’s a Dutch saying that translates to: a donkey doesn’t bump into the same stone twice — meaning that you can learn from mistakes you made in the past. When you are 90 years old, you can see whether you’ve been a donkey or not.

And a nine-year-old cares about three really important things: people, nature, the future. Nine-year-olds don’t have a lot of history, but they do have hope. They can be naive. They can dream. They can believe in things you lost all faith in.

Having history and hope together in one room, you can create the greatest things.

So let’s try — perhaps even close your eyes. Think back to when you were nine years old. Dream as you dreamed. Believe as you believed. And be the hero you wanted to be.

And imagine yourself as 90 years old. Judge yourself. Would you be proud? Would you be disappointed? Or are you a donkey who is running towards the same stone for the fifth time — or did you learn?

Because there are only two people you need to make proud in your life: your 90-year-old self, and your nine-year-old self. One is the story of history, the other a story of hope.

Thank you.
